AC HRA Portal v23 — Attendance automatic HRA Pay hours

- Attendance import no longer reads or requires Total Hours / Work Hours columns.
- Existing Attendance snapshots are sanitised so old manually imported hours cannot override the new source.
- Removed the visible Payroll / Meal comparison page and all manual Projection import/export/clear actions from Attendance.
- Attendance reads existing HRA Pay Payroll, Advance and Meal Fee Projection data automatically in the background.
- Hours priority: Payroll total/normal/OT hours, then Payroll work-days × 8, then Meal Fee meal-days × 8. Advance is not counted as work hours.
- Monthly and yearly hours are summed directly. Day and week views allocate monthly HRA Pay hours using Attendance daily attendance counts; department/section grouping is retained where available.
- If no usable HRA Pay hours are available, the Attendance Hours tab, Total Hours columns and total-hours KPIs hide automatically.
- No new workbook format, manual projection import, export, or user-side conversion is required.

Deployment:
- Upload the new portal ZIP and replace the existing Attendance HTML with ac_hra_attendance_v1.html.
- No new Google Apps Script action or GS deployment is required for this change.
- The existing HRA Pay Projection storage/API is read automatically; existing HRA Pay data remains the source.
